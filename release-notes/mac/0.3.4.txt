Audio now syncs with iCloud. With Sync with iCloud on, a recording made on your iPhone plays on your Mac, and the other way round, including voice sample clips. Audio is stored as a compressed copy, about 15 MB an hour, in your private iCloud, and moves over Wi-Fi or Ethernet. You can turn it off on each device in Settings → iCloud → Sync audio.

If you already sync with iCloud, Transcribe uploads the audio of your existing recordings once, newest first, after this update. Deleting a recording now also deletes its audio from iCloud. Devices on earlier versions keep syncing transcripts as before but don't upload or download audio.

Your Mac can now record the sound of calls and other apps, not just the microphone. Choose Microphone and Mac audio, or Mac audio only, in Settings → Recording or from the New Recording menu. macOS asks for permission the first time. Transcribe records sound only, never your screen, and works with headphones, including Bluetooth headsets on a call. Voices heard through call audio are learned separately from the same voices in the room.

Global shortcuts start and stop a recording (fn Z) and dictate into any app (fn X), even while Transcribe is in the background. Set them up in Settings → Shortcuts.

Transcribe upgrades its library the first time this version opens. Earlier versions cannot open the library afterwards. Update Transcribe on your iPhone to this version as well.
